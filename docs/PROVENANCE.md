# Provenance

This release candidate is a cleaned derivative of a larger private research archive on epistemic generalization in AI agents.

The private archive contains:
- abandoned and revised environment families;
- adversarial audits;
- frozen design gates;
- independent reproductions;
- archival recovery notes;
- the full project chronology.

Those materials are intentionally omitted here to keep the public artifact focused on the final methodological contribution.

## Publicly retained components

The release candidate retains only:
- the final Order-Necessity implementation;
- the positive control;
- DCR as a nonrepresentable-but-cheap negative example;
- compact K2/DCR result tables;
- the Bellman-regret interpretation;
- explicit limitations.

## Integrity principle

Historical negative results are not rewritten as positive findings.

The clean public presentation preserves these constraints:
- no confirmatory agent result is claimed;
- H1 is not described as rejected;
- DCR remains a design-level NO-GO under the historical threshold;
- ORDER_KEY remains identified as a synthetic positive control;
- no novelty claim is made.

## Reproduction status

The staged code was smoke-tested against retained reference values:
- ORDER_KEY_v1 gap: `0.5`;
- DCR frozen-grid maximum: `0.018933608625138487`.

This public candidate should be treated as a methodological snapshot, not as a peer-reviewed publication.
