# AI assistance disclosure

This project was developed with **substantial AI assistance**.

AI tools, including OpenAI ChatGPT, were used for tasks including methodological discussion, formalization assistance, code generation and review, adversarial critique, consistency checks, and documentation.

AI outputs were not treated as an automatic source of authority. The human author reviewed, questioned, and worked through generated analyses and proposals before making the corresponding research decisions.

The human author retained responsibility for:

- selecting the research question and scope;
- approving the structural definitions and evaluation logic;
- deciding which historical results were retained in the public artifact;
- interpreting the evidence and limitations;
- deciding the final claims and publication state.

The numerical results in this repository derive from the retained implementations and calculations, not from AI-generated substitute data. AI is not listed as an author.
